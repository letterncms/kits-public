This is a working blog, ready for your first story. Posts use the same blocks as pages, templates and components, while the article template gives the published text a comfortable reading width.

## Start with something useful

Replace this post with an answer your readers need. Keep the opening paragraph clear, then add examples, images or a list when they help explain the subject.

- Press `/` to insert a block.
- Drag a block by its handle to change the order.
- Open its settings for content, design and interactions.

> A useful page starts with a useful answer.
