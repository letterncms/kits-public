Saving and publishing are different steps. Saving protects the work in progress; publishing makes a reviewed version available to visitors.

## A simple review routine

1. Write the opening paragraph and add a clear title.
2. Preview the post with its article template.
3. Check links, image descriptions and the assigned author.
4. Publish now, or schedule an unpublished post for later.

## Keep improving a live post

Edits to a published post stay in a working copy until you publish the changes. You can leave a draft incomplete and return later without changing what visitors read.
