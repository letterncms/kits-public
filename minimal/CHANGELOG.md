## 0.2.0 (2026-11-13)

### Changed

- **Breaking.** Minimal is now a Site package. It uses `cms-site` and `site.json` and installs in `sites/minimal/`.

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-12)

### Changed

- The starter home page points to **Design** for colors, fonts and spacing.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-12)

### Added

- **Minimal as a kit of the store.** A home page, an about page and a blog with three posts, on one typeface and a calm design system: the site a new site starts from. It came with the CMS before; now `composer require letterncms/minimal` puts it in `kits/`, the store installs it, and it can be deleted when the site is another kit. `NOTICE.md` holds Inter's licence.
