We reached the bank at slack water and ran six lines across it. On the fourth the sounder dropped away and kept dropping. The lead agreed: 38.5 fathoms, twice what the January sheet shows.

A scour hole this deep does not last. We marked it, issued a notice the same evening and booked the boat again for May.
