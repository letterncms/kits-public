Gravel drains fast and unevenly. Two rows apart, one vine drinks and the next one starves. The grower wanted to plant by what the ground does, not by what the plan said.

We cored the plot on a ten-metre grid and logged moisture at three depths for two seasons. The second sheet is printed over the first in a second colour: you can see the dry tongue lengthen.

## What we measured

- Moisture at 30, 60 and 90 cm on a 10 m grid
- Gravel depth, by auger and patience
