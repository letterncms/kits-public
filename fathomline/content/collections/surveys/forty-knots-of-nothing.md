A beach looks flat. The air above it is not. The dunes at De Panne throw a wind shadow three hundred metres long, and inside it a kite simply falls.

We flew eight anemometer masts and a tethered sonde for the week before the festival, and drew the result as you would draw a seabed: lines of equal wind, with the dead air shaded like a shoal.

## What we measured

- Wind speed at 2, 10 and 40 metres on eight masts
- Turbulence behind the dune line, by sonde
- Where the sand actually went

The organisers used the sheet to lay out the flying fields. Nobody was put in the shadow.
