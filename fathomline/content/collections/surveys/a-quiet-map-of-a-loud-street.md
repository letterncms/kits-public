The Overpoort is four hundred metres of bars. The city wanted to know where the noise went after midnight, and the honest answer was: round the corner, and up.

Twenty-two loggers on lamp posts and in bedrooms gave us nine weeks of readings. We drew them as a set of hourly sheets, so the street can be read like a tide table.

## What we measured

- Sound level in dB(A) at 22 points, every second
- Which doors were open
