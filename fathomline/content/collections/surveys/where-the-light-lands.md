Architects model daylight. We went and stood in it. For a year a row of light meters sat on stakes in an empty courtyard in Antwerp, one reading a minute.

The sheet shows hours of direct sun as contours, month by month. The co-operative moved the play area eleven metres and the vegetable beds to the other side.

## What we measured

- Direct and diffuse light at 46 points for twelve months
- The shadow of a crane that stayed longer than planned
