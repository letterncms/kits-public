By day the concourse is a river. At night it is a few slow eddies: cleaners, a baker, the people waiting for the 04:52. The station wanted to relight it for them.

Counters over every doorway and a fortnight of nights gave us the flow lines. The lights now follow the eddies.

## What we measured

- Crossings at 31 thresholds, 23:00 to 06:00
- Where people stopped, and for how long
