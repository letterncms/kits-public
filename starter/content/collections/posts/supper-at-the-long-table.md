It started as a way to say thank you to the sawmill, the kiln repairman and the neighbours who put up with the noise. This year there were sixty-two of us, at nine tables of four different woods, eating from plates we threw over the summer.

::: image {"id":"st-supper","props":{"alt":"Friends talking around a table with wine, bread and vegetables.","image":["01m7ba38vbg5bzanb6w8nphrv2"],"caption":"Before anyone sat down."}}
:::

The menu is always the same: fish from the morning boat, potatoes from the farm behind the dunes, bread from the baker on the quay and whatever the garden has left in October. Nobody makes speeches. Somebody always sings.

::: image {"id":"st-bowl","props":{"alt":"Two people passing a bowl of food across a table.","image":["01m7ba38v9y5k4vr9x14mmp936"],"caption":"Bowls, passed hand to hand."}}
:::

Next year’s supper is on the last Saturday of October. If you would like to come, <a>write to us</a> in the summer and we will keep a seat.
