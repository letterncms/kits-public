Jonas has thrown pots in the loft since before it was a studio, when it still smelled of nets and tar. We sat with him on a Tuesday afternoon, between firings.

## How did you start?

With a wheel somebody left behind when they moved away. I taught myself from a library book and a lot of broken bowls. For the first year everything I made was too heavy. For the second year everything was too thin.

## What do you look for in clay?

Patience. Clay remembers everything you do to it, so if you rush the centring, the pot will tell you about it in the kiln three days later.

## The grey-green glaze is everywhere now. Where does it come from?

From the beach. We dry a bucket of sand from below the dune, sieve it fine and add it to a plain glaze. Every batch comes out a little different, which is what people like about it, and what drove me mad for the first ten years.

> A cup is finished when someone has used it every morning for a year. Before that it is only a good idea.

## Do you still throw every cup?

Every one. Mara and Yuna do the plates now, and they are better at it than I am. But I like to think a cup should come from one pair of hands.
